# Design guidance sources

The craft guidance and archetype cards are written for first-draft's script-free HTML exports. They are not verbatim imported skills. We adapted interaction, motion and resilient-layout principles and subject-led planning; interactive skill wrappers, JavaScript techniques and external assets are intentionally omitted.

- **Emil Kowalski**, [emilkowalski/skills](https://github.com/emilkowalski/skills/tree/2d228630ba1446365f614a108cb8252f1feda202): `emil-design-eng`, `mobile-native`, `break-ui`. MIT, Copyright (c) 2026 Emil Kowalski. [Retained license](./licenses/emil-MIT.txt).
- **Anthropic**, [frontend-design](https://github.com/anthropics/skills/tree/main/skills/frontend-design) only: subject-specific direction, palette/type/layout planning, restraint and useful copy. Apache-2.0; [retained license](./licenses/anthropic-Apache-2.0.txt). Our text is a condensed rewrite for a headless, no-network generator, not the original skill. No other Anthropic skill is included.
- Card field structure was informed by the category-to-design idea in [UI UX Pro Max](https://github.com/nextlevelbuilder/ui-ux-pro-max-skill) (MIT); no CSV rows, code or font data are included. The persuade/operate/read distinction is also a conceptual influence from [Impeccable](https://github.com/pbakaus/impeccable) (Apache-2.0); no content is copied.

Reference brands in the cards are verbal mood comparisons only. Their palettes, tokens, logos and assets are not sourced or reproduced. Attribution is not an endorsement by any author or brand. Emil's course and blog are not included.
