# Studio: one option with desktop 1280 and phone 390 side by side (or one device), beside its brief panel

Part of the first-draft verification map. Source: `src/view/app.js` (`canvas`, `switcher`, `brief`, `send`), `src/view.mjs` (the `/refine` route), `src/cli.mjs` (`refineHtml`), `src/view/canvas.js` (`deviceFor`, `fitDevice`), `src/view/view.css` (Studio, the fonts in `src/view/fonts/`).

## How to get to it (user POV)

From the grid, click an option card, or open <url>#/o/1. The viewport switch opens <url>#/o/1/both (wide windows only), <url>#/o/1/desktop and <url>#/o/1/phone. Add `?screen=<id>` before the hash to show that same screen in both frames, e.g. `<url>?screen=detail#/o/1/both`. It is initial preview state, not a modified export; the sandbox/CSP remain unchanged.

## Driving it

Harness: A shell runs the CLI and curl for the url. A browser tool opens the url and each #/o/<n> route for screenshots, with prefers-color-scheme set to light and to dark.

1. Launch the app as SKILL.md says, with a `<design>.comments.json` next to the design so the panel lists comments, and wait until it is ready.
2. Browser at 1280 wide: open <url>#/o/1. One top bar: the panel toggle (labelled Hide brief), the logo and the design's name (both open the grid), and the option switcher (the open option's number and label; its menu lists every option with its comment count, an option being made, and "All options side by side"); in the centre Both/Desktop/Phone with icons and the zoom as a percentage; on the right Comment, Fullscreen (an icon) and Play, the one filled (ink) button. The panel on the left lists the options and the open option's comments ("No comments yet. Press Comment, then click a spot on the design." when there are none) above the brief composer. With no device in the route the Studio opens on Desktop where Both cannot be drawn at 83% or more (`BOTH_MIN`: 1280 and 1440 with the panel open), on Both where it can (1920), or on the device this viewer last picked on a wide screen (kept in the browser). Both shows the desktop and the phone side by side at one scale, each captioned with its name, size and its own Play, at the largest scale that fits the canvas (fit to space, not capped at 100%: it covers at least 85% of the canvas available to it on its limiting side, the canvas less the comment-pin margins when pins show); no pin covers either frame. Press the panel toggle: the panel and the composer fold away, the canvas takes the full width and the toggle is labelled Show brief; the panel stays folded across Phone/Desktop until Show brief brings it back. Under 860px there is no panel toggle.
3. Press Phone, then Desktop: only that device shows, filling the canvas the same way, the route changes to #/o/1/phone or #/o/1/desktop, and Play links to #/o/1/play/<device>. Exit in Play goes back to the Studio route it was opened from.
4. Click a comment in the panel: its numbered pin opens on the canvas. Click another option in the panel or in the switcher's menu: the canvas switches and keeps the device.
5. Brief, send: with an agent set (`init --agent byokit:<route> --model <model>`; the proof's agent is a provider stand-in at the model edge via `FIRST_DRAFT_TEST_BYOKIT_BASE`, so no paid call), type a change and press Send (the round arrow). The composer shows a card with a spinner, "Making Option N from Option 1" and the seconds so far, Send turns into a spinner, and the panel and the switcher list the option being made; when it ends the new option (`<option>-refined.html`, beside the original) is in the Options list and the picker, and the canvas switches to it with "Option N is ready" and the change in the card. With no agent set, Send shows a red card with the set-up hint, its `first-draft-axi init --agent byokit:<route> ...` command as a code block, and makes no model call. "Terminal command" in the composer shows the `first-draft-axi refine ...` command with Copy.
6. Browser at 390 wide: open <url>#/o/1 (or #/o/1/both). The bar is two rows: the logo, the option switcher (its menu opens as a sheet at the foot of the screen), Play (the one filled button) and More (⋯) on the first; Desktop/Phone (no Both) and the zoom on the second. The canvas comes next and shows the phone mock alone (never a desktop pane under it) as large as it fits; the panel follows below, and the brief is docked at the bottom of the screen. Fullscreen and Add comment are in the More menu (a sheet; Escape or a tap outside closes it). The docked brief is one line (the text and Send) and the whole phone shows above it; focused or written in, it opens up and shows Terminal command.
7. Press states: holding the mouse (or a finger) on any button or button-like link (Play, Send, Desktop/Phone, More and its Fullscreen, the option switcher, the design name, the panel toggle, an option or comment row, Terminal command, Exit) changes its look until release.
8. Scripted: `node journeys/studio.mjs <evidence folder>` (or `done-done run studio`) runs steps 2-7 and the comment feature's add-a-comment step on a two-option design made from `journeys/fixtures/notes.html`, or the first two HTML files of a real run's folder with `FD_STUDIO_SOURCE=<folder>` (copied into a temporary folder, never changed), at 1280 and 390, light and dark, in headless Chromium with no agent set and no model call. It saves one capture per claim and theme to the evidence folder: `studio-{1280,390}-{light,dark}.png` (the whole Studio as it opens), `canvas-both-1280.png` (desktop and phone side by side, light and dark), `add-comment-1280-{light,dark}.png` (the comment box at the tapped point, then the saved pin), `add-comment-390.png` (the same at 390, light and dark), `hide-brief-1280-{light,dark}.png` (before and after Hide brief, side by side) and, at 390 with light and dark side by side in each, `phone-row-390.png` (the bar and the whole phone above the one-line brief), `more-menu-390.png` (More open), `press-play-390.png` (Play at rest and held) and `composer-390.png` (the brief at rest and tapped), plus `studio-states.json` and `steps.json`; the single captures (Send, Play, More, the tapped brief) go to `$FD_EXTRA_OUT` when set. It checks that each device and Play cover at least 85% of the canvas available to them on its limiting side (the canvas less the comment-pin margins when pins show), captures Both, Desktop and Phone at 1280 and 1440 wide, light and dark, as `fit-{both,desktop,phone}-{1280,1440}-{light,dark}.png` with `fit.json` (zoom and cover), checks the default device at 1280, 1440 and 1920 (`default-{1280,1440,1920}-light.png`) and that a picked device is remembered. It ends with `PASS:` or fails on the first broken step.
9. Capture: screenshot each state at 390 and 1280, light and dark, to $DONE_DONE_EVIDENCE_ROOT/, as SKILL.md's Evidence section says.
10. Stop what you started.
