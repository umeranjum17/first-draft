# Studio: one option with desktop 1280 and phone 390 side by side (or one device), beside its brief column

Part of the first-draft verification map. Source: `src/view/app.js` (`canvas`, `brief`, `send`), `src/view.mjs` (the `/refine` route), `src/cli.mjs` (`refineHtml`), `src/view/canvas.js` (`deviceFor`, `fitDevice`), `src/view/view.css` (Studio).

## How to get to it (user POV)

From the grid, click an option card, or open <url>#/o/1. The viewport switch opens <url>#/o/1/both (wide windows only), <url>#/o/1/desktop and <url>#/o/1/phone.

## Driving it

Harness: A shell runs the CLI and curl for the url. A browser tool opens the url and each #/o/<n> route for screenshots, with prefers-color-scheme set to light and to dark.

1. Launch the app as SKILL.md says, with a `<design>.comments.json` next to the design so the column lists comments, and wait until it is ready.
2. Browser at 1280 wide: open <url>#/o/1. Check the column on the left (design name, option list, the open option's comments, the Next brief composer) and the dotted canvas on the right showing the desktop and the phone side by side at one scale (Both is the current switch segment, the zoom reads "Side by side"), each captioned with its name, size and its own Play; no pin covers either frame. Press Hide brief in the top bar: the column and the composer fold away, the canvas takes the full width and the button reads Show brief; the column stays folded across Phone/Desktop until Show brief brings it back. Under 860px there is no Hide brief button.
3. Press Phone, then Desktop: only that device shows, the route changes to #/o/1/phone or #/o/1/desktop, and Play links to #/o/1/play/<device>. Exit in Play goes back to the Studio route it was opened from.
4. Click a comment in the column: its numbered pin opens on the canvas. Click another option in the list: the canvas switches and keeps the device.
5. Next brief, send: with an agent set (`init --agent byokit:<route> --model <model>`; the proof's agent is a provider stand-in at the model edge via `FIRST_DRAFT_TEST_BYOKIT_BASE`, so no paid call), type a change and press Send. The box shows a spinner and "Making a new option from Option 1" with the seconds so far, Send reads "Working…"; when it ends the new option (`<option>-refined.html`, beside the original) is in the Options list and the picker, and the canvas switches to it with "Option 2 is Option 1 with this change: ..." in the box. With no agent set, Send shows the set-up hint (`first-draft-axi init --agent byokit:<route> ...`) in red in the box and makes no model call. "Copy terminal command" stays a small link under the text: it shows the `first-draft-axi refine ...` command with Copy.
6. Browser at 390 wide: open <url>#/o/1 (or #/o/1/both). The canvas comes first and shows the phone mock alone (never a desktop pane under it, no Both in the switch), the option picker is a select above the switch, the column follows below, and the brief is docked at the bottom of the screen. The control row is one line: Desktop/Phone, a More button (⋯) and Play, the one primary; Fullscreen and Add comment are in the More menu (Escape or a tap outside closes it). The docked brief is one line (the text and Send) and the whole phone shows above it; focused or written in, it opens up and shows Copy terminal command.
7. Press states: holding the mouse (or a finger) on any button or button-like link (Play, Send, Desktop/Phone, More and its Fullscreen, All options, Hide brief, an option or comment row, Copy terminal command, Exit) changes its look until release; Play turns the accent colour (orange).
8. Scripted: `node journeys/studio.mjs <evidence folder>` (or `done-done run` with the `studio` journey in `.done-done.yaml`) runs steps 2-7 and the comment feature's add-a-comment step on a two-option design made from `journeys/fixtures/notes.html` at 1280 and 390, light and dark, in headless Chromium with no agent set and no model call, and saves one capture per claim and theme to the evidence folder: `canvas-both-1280.png` (desktop and phone side by side, light and dark), `add-comment-1280-{light,dark}.png` (the comment box at the tapped point, then the saved pin), `add-comment-390.png` (the same at 390, light and dark), `hide-brief-1280-{light,dark}.png` (the 1280 Studio before and after Hide brief, side by side) and, at 390 with light and dark side by side in each, `phone-row-390.png` (the control row and the whole phone above the one-line brief), `more-menu-390.png` (More open), `press-play-390.png` (Play at rest and held) and `composer-390.png` (the brief at rest and tapped), plus `studio-states.json` (each state's measured width, theme, column and composer); the single captures (each width and theme, Send, Play, More, the tapped brief) go to `$FD_EXTRA_OUT` when set. It ends with `PASS:` or fails on the first broken step.
9. Capture: screenshot each state at 390 and 1280, light and dark, to $DONE_DONE_EVIDENCE_ROOT/, as SKILL.md's Evidence section says.
10. Stop what you started.

The drive changes no repo file: note findings in the run's report; after the drive, run done-done maintain to see what drifted, then fix the skill.
