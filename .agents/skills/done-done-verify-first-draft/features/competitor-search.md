# Competitor search: what the market does beside the options

Sources: `src/reference-competitors.mjs`, `src/reference-capture.mjs`, `src/reference-network.mjs`, `src/reference-terms.mjs`, `src/cli.mjs`, `src/view.mjs`, `src/view/app.js`, `src/view/view.css`, `journeys/references.mjs`.

## Drive

1. Deterministic, no network: `npm run build && node --test test/public-references.test.mjs`. The local fixture site serves the stand-in competitor pages; the stand-in CLI answers `name_market_competitors` with five candidates. Assertions: three are captured, one is refused by a restrictive terms page and one by robots (`competitors.refused` with each reason), every competitor carries its own live source URL, the two-option `<option>.html.competitors.json` shapes and PNG dimensions match, a refusal page is never screenshotted, fewer than three candidates stops the run before any fetch, and `--competitors` conflicts with `--like` and `--no-references`.
2. Real run (needs spending approval and an installed signed-in model route): `first-draft-axi make "<a product brief that names no brand>" --competitors --options 1 --no-critic --out <out.html> --model <id>`. Read the make JSON: `competitors.captured` (name, live url, permission terms URL) and `competitors.refused` (name, url, why). Every captured entry must have a URL the capture actually fetched; a refusal must never appear in `captured` and nothing may be drawn in its place.
3. Sealed viewer proof, nonpaid: retain the real `<out.html>` with its `.refs.json` and `.competitors.json` sidecars, then `FD_REFERENCE_SOURCE=<out.html> done-done run references`. With both sidecars the journey captures four `references-<width>-<theme>.png` frames and four `market-<width>-<theme>.png` frames (390/1280 × light/dark). The market frames assert the section exists, every competitor image decoded, every name is a live source link, and no horizontal overflow.
4. Open every PNG before retaining it: each competitor shows its name, its own host, its Source/Permission links and its captures without overlap or clipped text; the refusals list stays visible with a reason each. A refusal frame is not evidence that the site refused; the JSON reason is.

## Boundaries

Competitor discovery is the configured model's opinion of who the market is: real URLs are required, a missing one drops the candidate, and fewer than three valid candidates stops the run instead of padding the list. Capture is the existing anonymous path only (public DNS pinned, robots + terms review, no cookies/query strings/custom ports/login/challenges, 32 requests, 2 MB/resource, 8 MB total, 60 s/capture); there is no new scraper, no bypass and no signed-in session. Terms review is a model assessment, not legal advice, and many real competitors will be refused. Competitors are viewer context, not model inputs, and never design assets. A test pass or a fixture response does not prove live internet reachability or policy correctness; a nonpaid recapture performs no new generation.
