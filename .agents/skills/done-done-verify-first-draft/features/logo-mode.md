## Assert and capture

`journeys/logo.mjs` checks each option's folder (SVG roots, `palette.json`, PNG sizes from the IHDR, favicon entries equal the PNGs, the critic's boards, `logo.json` full-bleed with concept, explored concepts, critique and palette) and that each sheet carries its idea and palette, and the "Did not pass the self-critique" line exactly when `passed` is false. It then captures, with an owned chrome-devtools-axi browser per width and theme: `picker-<360|390|445|1280|1440>-<light|dark>.png` (every option in the picker) and `studio-<n>-<360|390|445|1280|1440>-<light|dark>.png` (each option's brand sheet in the Studio: name, idea and palette in its first screen), `explored.png` (every explored concept with idea, kind, where in the brief it comes from, base shape, critic's reading, verdict and status, and the boards the critic saw, one band per territory), and per option `critique-<n>.png` (concept, palette with contrast, each critique round, the 16 px detail numbers and the critic's board), `icons-<n>.png` (every icon PNG at its real size) and `svgs-<n>.png` (each SVG alone on the tile it is for), plus `steps.json`. Hashes, the evidence pages and measurements go to the sibling `-support` folder. Open every capture: the marks read, the idea and palette show in the sheet's first screen, the -dark files read on the dark tiles, the 16 px icon is not a blob, nothing overlaps or is cut off. Mark quality is a judgement the checks do not make: write it down honestly, against the previous run.

## Boundaries

The checks are structural: an allow-list, viewBox, size, palette colours and their 3:1 contrast, full-bleed, a safe-zone and 16 px ink/contrast measure, the 16 px detail measure (parts at 512 px against the 16 px render and box-downsample, and features narrower than one 16 px pixel by morphological opening), and 3:1 on light and dark tiles. The critique requires an authenticated engine and model independent of the generator, against written principles and the person's `--ref` images. A missing or failed judge refuses output; this is not a look-alike check or trademark clearance. No inspiration site is fetched or captured; the reference policy is unchanged. No font outlining. Image-first exploration uses the asset providers' own logins (Codex subscription quota, Higgsfield credits); no key is read or sent anywhere new.
