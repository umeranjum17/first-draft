Entry: `src/cli.mjs logo` (also `styles --logo`, and `refine` / the Studio brief on a logo option), `src/logo-concepts.mjs` (concept exploration, the critic, selection, redo and drop), `src/logo.mjs` (export contract, `done` checks, the 16 px detail measure, palette, sheet slots), `src/make.mjs`, `src/render-check.mjs` (`evaluate`), `templates/skills/logo-system.md`; deterministic journey `test/logo.test.mjs`; nonpaid recapture `journeys/logo.mjs`.

## Assert and capture

`journeys/logo.mjs` checks each option's folder (SVG roots, `palette.json`, PNG sizes from the IHDR, favicon entries equal the PNGs, the critic's boards, `logo.json` full-bleed with concept, explored concepts, critique and palette) and that each sheet carries its idea and palette, and the "Did not pass the self-critique" line exactly when `passed` is false. It then captures, with an owned chrome-devtools-axi browser per width and theme: `picker-<360|390|445|1280|1440>-<light|dark>.png` (every option in the picker) and `studio-<n>-<360|390|445|1280|1440>-<light|dark>.png` (each option's brand sheet in the Studio: name, idea and palette in its first screen), `explored.png` (every explored concept with idea, kind, critic's reading, verdict and status, and the board the critic saw), and per option `critique-<n>.png` (concept, palette with contrast, each critique round, the 16 px detail numbers and the critic's board), `icons-<n>.png` (every icon PNG at its real size) and `svgs-<n>.png` (each SVG alone on the tile it is for), plus `steps.json`. Hashes, the evidence pages and measurements go to the sibling `-support` folder. Open every capture: the marks read, the idea and palette show in the sheet's first screen, the -dark files read on the dark tiles, the 16 px icon is not a blob, nothing overlaps or is cut off. Mark quality is a judgement the checks do not make: write it down honestly, against the previous run.

## Boundaries

The checks are structural: an allow-list, viewBox, size, palette colours and their 3:1 contrast, full-bleed, a safe-zone and 16 px ink/contrast measure, the 16 px detail measure (parts at 512 px against the 16 px render and box-downsample, and features narrower than one 16 px pixel by morphological opening), and 3:1 on light and dark tiles. The critique is the same model judging its own work against written principles and the person's `--ref` images; it is not an independent judgement, a look-alike check or trademark clearance. No inspiration site is fetched or captured; the reference policy is unchanged. No font outlining or raster exploration.
