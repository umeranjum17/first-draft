How it works

Make, compare, refine.

$ first-draft-axi make "A notes app" --out notes.html
$ first-draft-axi view notes.html
{"viewing":{"url":"http://127.0.0.1:4430/", …}}

01Start with a prompt

Ask for a screen or a flow. You get three directions, each in its own style, instead of committing to the first one; --options 1 to 5 sets how many.

$ first-draft-axi view notes.html
{"viewing":{"url":"http://127.0.0.1:4430/",
 "options":[{"option":1,"file":"notes-1.html", …}, …]}}

02See what changes

Every option side by side. Open one in the Studio to see its desktop and phone at once, and click a spot to comment on it.

$ first-draft-axi refine notes-2.html "warmer, with an empty state" --out notes-warm.html
notes-2.html stays as it was

03Keep the good direction

Describe a change and your agent writes it as a new option. The one you started from stays untouched.

Get started

Your first draft, in three steps.

  1. npm install -g first-draft-axi
  2. first-draft-axi init --agent byokit:claude --model <model>
  3. first-draft-axi make "A notes app" --out notes.html && first-draft-axi view notes.html

Node 22.18+. Step 2 uses your Claude subscription (Pro or Max) through your signed-in Claude CLI; put a Claude model it supports in place of <model>. On ChatGPT, use byokit:codex with your signed-in Codex CLI. Other model routes. Open the URL that view prints.

Not just a picture

A design you can open, at every width.

Each option is a self-contained HTML file. The Studio shows it at desktop and phone width beside its brief; Play clicks through its screens and Fullscreen gives it the whole window.

  • Every screen is checked at 390 and 1280, light and dark.
  • Viewing needs no model call, on a laptop or a phone.
  • Comments are saved next to the design as plain JSON.

Works alone, or with sign-off.

first-draft needs nothing else. To get someone's pick, hand the options to sign-off, a sibling review board; each card opens the option in Play here.

first-draft-axi post notes.html --title "Which notes app?" --recommend a

Hand it to your coding agent.

The CLI prints JSON, so an agent can run make and view on its own. Give it the agent skill and it knows the commands.

first-draft-axi --help
